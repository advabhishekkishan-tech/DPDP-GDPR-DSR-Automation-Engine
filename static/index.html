PRACTITIONER WORKSPACE · START HERE

Turn a privacy request into a reasoned, reviewable decision.

Use this lab to practise the legal and operational steps behind a Data Subject Request (DSR), review consent events, document legal reasoning and screen vendor privacy risks.

Demonstration only: connected systems are simulated. Use synthetic information, never real client or customer data.

How to use the lab

  1. Create a case.
    Choose the request type, legal framework and identity-assurance level.
  2. Read the result.
    Review the simulated systems, proposed actions, verification and audit evidence.
  3. Apply legal judgement.
    Check the right, applicability, exceptions, retention and evidence before approving an outcome.
  4. Control downstream action.
    Use legal review before processor propagation; do not treat a successful API response as proof of legal compliance.
  5. Preserve the record.
    Use the evidence and audit trail to explain what happened, why, and what remains unresolved.

What do the case results mean?

Proposed action
What the demo workflow attempted, not a legal ruling.
Verified
Whether the simulated post-action check matched the expected result.
Legal review / approval
A human control point for recording an approval or rejection before high-impact processing.
Audit integrity
Whether the demo's hash-linked event record passes its integrity check. It does not independently prove that the underlying facts are true.
Processor propagation
A simulated downstream instruction/acknowledgement workflow, not a live command to a real vendor.
